Privacy Policy (English)

Published by Akhor Khel, an individual developer (“the developer”, “we”). Contact: akhorkhel.app@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Akhor Khel is a learning app for children aged 3 to 8.
  • The developer does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no server, no analytics and no chat.
  • Children’s progress stays only on the device, and a parent can delete it at any time.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are requested as child-directed and non-personalised, limited to general-audience (G) content. To show ads, Google’s ad software in the app sends some technical data to Google, described below.

1. Information stored on the device

The app saves the following on the device only. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else by the app:

  • child profiles: a nickname and a chosen avatar picture (no real name, age, photo or other personal detail is asked for);
  • learning progress: stars, attempts and completed letters;
  • settings: language, sound, left-hand mode and similar;
  • ad counters and the expiry time of any reward unlocked in the parent area.

The child’s tracing is used only on the device to give stars and is never sent anywhere. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Teacher export. In teacher mode (behind the parent gate), an adult can create a PDF or CSV of class progress (nicknames and stars). The file is created on the device and only leaves it if the adult chooses to share it using Android’s share menu, to an app and recipient the adult selects.

2. Information processed by Google AdMob

When the device is online, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app requests and shows ads. Google states that this SDK collects the following, for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes, and that it is encrypted in transit:

  • the device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate the device’s general (approximate) location;
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, ad taps and ad video views;
  • diagnostic information about the app’s and the SDK’s performance, such as launch time;
  • device identifiers such as the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers.

What we do to protect children:

  • every ad request is marked as child-directed, which tells Google to turn off interest-based (personalised) advertising and remarketing for that request;
  • only non-personalised ads are requested, with a maximum ad content rating of G;
  • the app does not use the Android advertising ID: the permission that gives access to it is removed from the app, and child-directed requests do not transmit it;
  • the app uses only Google AdMob, which is on Google Play’s list of Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs, and no other advertising, analytics or tracking software;
  • ads never appear when the app opens or while a child is tracing or playing a learning game. Optional reward ads are offered only to adults, behind the parent gate.

Google processes this data under its own policies. Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

The developer does not receive these data from Google except as aggregated, non-identifying reports (for example, total number of ad impressions and earnings). If the device is offline, no ad requests are made and every lesson still works.

3. Children’s privacy

Akhor Khel is designed for children and is built to meet Google Play’s Families policy.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone. The app has no way to send the developer any information about a child.
  • We do not track children across apps or websites, build profiles of them, or show them personalised or behavioural ads. The ad data described in section 2 is processed by Google for child-directed, non-personalised ads only.
  • Features meant for adults (settings, teacher mode, sharing, optional reward ads and links that leave the app) are behind a parent gate.
  • United States (COPPA): the app is intended to be handled as a child-directed service. Ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment so that Google applies its COPPA-related restrictions. The developer does not collect personal information from children.
  • India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025): the developer does not process children’s personal data and does not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children. Ads are requested in child-directed, non-personalised mode. Before the children’s obligations under the Rules apply, the developer will review this policy and the app with legal advice, and update both if anything more is needed.

Parents with questions can contact us at the email below.

4. Deleting information

Because the developer holds no information about you or your child, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Parents can remove the on-device information at any time:

  • in the app: Parent area → delete a child profile, or delete all progress on this device;
  • in Android: Settings → Apps → Akhor Khel → Storage → Clear storage; or
  • by uninstalling the app.

App backups are switched off, so progress is not copied to cloud backups by the app. For data held by Google in connection with ads, see Google’s privacy controls.

5. Permissions

The app asks Android for only two permissions, both granted automatically and used only by the ad software: Internet and network state (to know whether an ad can be loaded). It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, notifications or the advertising ID.

6. Security

There is no server or database to breach. Data on the device is stored in the app’s private storage. Communication between the ads SDK and Google is encrypted (TLS).

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s update notes.

১. ডিভাইচত থকা তথ্য

শিশুৰ ডাকনাম আৰু বাছি লোৱা ছবি, তৰা আৰু অগ্ৰগতি, ছেটিং, বিজ্ঞাপনৰ গণনা আৰু পুৰস্কাৰৰ সময় — এইবোৰ কেৱল ডিভাইচতে থাকে; এপে এইবোৰ ডেভেলপাৰ বা আন কাৰোলৈ নপঠিয়ায়। শিশুৰ আখৰ লিখা কেৱল ডিভাইচতে তৰা দিবলৈ ব্যৱহাৰ হয়। এপে কেমেৰা, মাইক্ৰ’ফোন, সম্পৰ্ক, অৱস্থান বা ফাইল ব্যৱহাৰ নকৰে। শিক্ষক অংশত প্ৰাপ্তবয়স্কই PDF বা CSV বনাব পাৰে; প্ৰাপ্তবয়স্কই নিজে শ্বেয়াৰ কৰিলেহে সেয়া ডিভাইচৰ বাহিৰলৈ যায়।

২. Google AdMobএ ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা তথ্য

ডিভাইচ অনলাইন থাকিলে এপৰ ভিতৰৰ Google Mobile Ads SDKএ বিজ্ঞাপন আনে। Googleৰ মতে এই SDKএ বিজ্ঞাপন, বিশ্লেষণ আৰু প্ৰতাৰণা ৰোধৰ বাবে তলৰ তথ্য সংগ্ৰহ কৰে আৰু সেয়া এনক্ৰিপ্ট কৰি পঠিয়ায়: IP ঠিকনা (যাৰ পৰা আনুমানিক অৱস্থান অনুমান কৰিব পাৰি), বিজ্ঞাপন আৰু এপৰ সৈতে কৰা কাম (যেনে এপ খোলা, বিজ্ঞাপন টিপা), কাৰ্যক্ষমতাৰ তথ্য, আৰু app set IDৰ দৰে ডিভাইচ চিনাক্তকাৰী।

প্ৰতিটো বিজ্ঞাপন অনুৰোধ শিশু-নিৰ্দেশিত বুলি চিহ্নিত কৰা হয়, কেৱল ব্যক্তিগতকৃত নোহোৱা আৰু G-মানৰ বিজ্ঞাপন বিচৰা হয়। এপে Android advertising ID ব্যৱহাৰ নকৰে। Google AdMobৰ বাহিৰে আন কোনো বিজ্ঞাপন, এনালিটিক্স বা ট্ৰেকিং ছফ্টৱেৰ নাই। এপ খোলোঁতে বা শিশুৱে লিখি বা খেলি থকা সময়ত বিজ্ঞাপন নাহে; পুৰস্কাৰ-বিজ্ঞাপন কেৱল অভিভাৱকৰ দুৱাৰৰ পিছফালে প্ৰাপ্তবয়স্কক দিয়া হয়। Googleএ নিজৰ নীতি অনুসৰি এই তথ্য ব্যৱহাৰ কৰে: Google গোপনীয়তা নীতি।

৩. শিশুৰ গোপনীয়তা

আমি জানি-শুনি কাৰো পৰা ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য সংগ্ৰহ নকৰোঁ। আমি শিশুক ট্ৰেক নকৰোঁ, তেওঁলোকৰ প্ৰ’ফাইল নবনাওঁ আৰু ব্যক্তিগতকৃত বা আচৰণভিত্তিক বিজ্ঞাপন নেদেখুৱাওঁ। ভাৰতৰ ডিজিটেল ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য সুৰক্ষা আইন, ২০২৩ আৰু নিয়মাৱলী, ২০২৫ অনুসৰি ডেভেলপাৰে শিশুৰ ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য প্ৰক্ৰিয়া নকৰে আৰু শিশুক লক্ষ্য কৰি ট্ৰেকিং, আচৰণ নিৰীক্ষণ বা লক্ষ্যভিত্তিক বিজ্ঞাপন নকৰে। নিয়মাৱলীৰ শিশু-সম্পৰ্কীয় বাধ্যবাধকতা কাৰ্যকৰী হোৱাৰ আগতে আইনী পৰামৰ্শ লৈ এই নীতি পুনৰীক্ষণ কৰা হ’ব।
